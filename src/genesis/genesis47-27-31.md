# Genesis 47:27-31 The Gospel Presence

## The Text

>Genesis 47:27–31  
><sup>27</sup> Israel settled in the land of Egypt, in the region of Goshen. They acquired property in it and became fruitful and very numerous. 
>
><sup>28</sup> Now Jacob lived in the land of Egypt 17 years, and his life span was 147 years. 
>
><sup>29</sup> When the time approached for him to die, he called his son Joseph and said to him, “If I have found favor with you, put your hand under my thigh and promise me that you will deal with me in kindness and faithfulness. Do not bury me in Egypt. 
>
><sup>30</sup> When I rest with my ancestors, carry me away from Egypt and bury me in their burial place.” Joseph answered, “I will do what you have asked.” 
>
><sup>31</sup> And Jacob said, “Swear to me.” So Joseph swore to him. Then Israel bowed in thanks at the head of his bed.
