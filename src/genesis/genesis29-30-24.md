# Genesis 29-30:24 The Gospel Inheritance

## The Text

>Genesis 29–30:24  
><sup>1</sup> Jacob resumed his journey and went to the eastern country. 
>
><sup>2</sup> He looked and saw a well in a field. Three flocks of sheep were lying there beside it because the sheep were watered from this well. But a large stone covered the opening of the well. 
>
><sup>3</sup> The shepherds would roll the stone from the opening of the well and water the sheep when all the flocks were gathered there. Then they would return the stone to its place over the well’s opening. 
>
><sup>4</sup> Jacob asked the men at the well, “My brothers! Where are you from?” “We’re from Haran,” they answered. 
>
><sup>5</sup> “Do you know Laban, Nahor’s grandson?” Jacob asked them. They answered, “We know him.” 
>
><sup>6</sup> “Is he well?” Jacob asked. “Yes,” they said, “and here is his daughter Rachel, coming with his sheep.” 
>
><sup>7</sup> Then Jacob said, “Look, it is still broad daylight. It’s not time for the animals to be gathered. Water the flock, then go out and let them graze.” 
>
><sup>8</sup> But they replied, “We can’t until all the flocks have been gathered and the stone is rolled from the well’s opening. Then we will water the sheep.” 
>
><sup>9</sup> While he was still speaking with them, Rachel came with her father’s sheep, for she was a shepherdess. 
>
><sup>10</sup> As soon as Jacob saw his uncle Laban’s daughter Rachel with his sheep, he went up and rolled the stone from the opening and watered his uncle Laban’s sheep. 
>
><sup>11</sup> Then Jacob kissed Rachel and wept loudly. 
>
><sup>12</sup> He told Rachel that he was her father’s relative, Rebekah’s son. She ran and told her father.Jacob Deceived 
>
><sup>13</sup> When Laban heard the news about his sister’s son Jacob, he ran to meet him, hugged him, and kissed him. Then he took him to his house, and Jacob told him all that had happened. 
>
><sup>14</sup> Laban said to him, “Yes, you are my own flesh and blood.” After Jacob had stayed with him a month, 
>
><sup>15</sup> Laban said to him, “Just because you’re my relative, should you work for me for nothing? Tell me what your wages should be.” 
>
><sup>16</sup> Now Laban had two daughters: the older was named Leah, and the younger was named Rachel. 
>
><sup>17</sup> Leah had tender eyes, but Rachel was shapely and beautiful. 
>
><sup>18</sup> Jacob loved Rachel, so he answered Laban, “I’ll work for you seven years for your younger daughter Rachel.” 
>
><sup>19</sup> Laban replied, “Better that I give her to you than to some other man. Stay with me.” 
>
><sup>20</sup> So Jacob worked seven years for Rachel, and they seemed like only a few days to him because of his love for her. 
>
><sup>21</sup> Then Jacob said to Laban, “Since my time is complete, give me my wife, so I can sleep with her.” 
>
><sup>22</sup> So Laban invited all the men of the place and sponsored a feast. 
>
><sup>23</sup> That evening, Laban took his daughter Leah and gave her to Jacob, and he slept with her. 
>
><sup>24</sup> And Laban gave his slave Zilpah to his daughter Leah as her slave. 
>
><sup>25</sup> When morning came, there was Leah! So he said to Laban, “What have you done to me? Wasn’t it for Rachel that I worked for you? Why have you deceived me?” 
>
><sup>26</sup> Laban answered, “It is not the custom in our country to give the younger daughter in marriage before the firstborn. 
>
><sup>27</sup> Complete this week of wedding celebration, and we will also give you this younger one in return for working yet another seven years for me.” 
>
><sup>28</sup> And Jacob did just that. He finished the week of celebration, and Laban gave him his daughter Rachel as his wife. 
>
><sup>29</sup> And Laban gave his slave Bilhah to his daughter Rachel as her slave. 
>
><sup>30</sup> Jacob slept with Rachel also, and indeed, he loved Rachel more than Leah. And he worked for Laban another seven years.Jacob’s Sons 
>
><sup>31</sup> When the Lord saw that Leah was neglected, he opened her womb; but Rachel was unable to conceive. 
>
><sup>32</sup> Leah conceived, gave birth to a son, and named him Reuben, for she said, “The Lord has seen my affliction; surely my husband will love me now.” 
>
><sup>33</sup> She conceived again, gave birth to a son, and said, “The Lord heard that I am neglected and has given me this son also.” So she named him Simeon. 
>
><sup>34</sup> She conceived again, gave birth to a son, and said, “At last, my husband will become attached to me because I have borne three sons for him.” Therefore he was named Levi. 
>
><sup>35</sup> And she conceived again, gave birth to a son, and said, “This time I will praise the Lord.” Therefore she named him Judah. Then Leah stopped having children. 
>
>Genesis 30  
><sup>1</sup> When Rachel saw that she was not bearing Jacob any children, she envied her sister. “Give me sons, or I will die!” she said to Jacob. 
>
><sup>2</sup> Jacob became angry with Rachel and said, “Am I in the place of God? He has withheld offspring from you!” 
>
><sup>3</sup> Then she said, “Here is my maid Bilhah. Go sleep with her, and she’ll bear children for me so that through her I too can build a family.” 
>
><sup>4</sup> So Rachel gave her slave Bilhah to Jacob as a wife, and he slept with her. 
>
><sup>5</sup> Bilhah conceived and bore Jacob a son. 
>
><sup>6</sup> Rachel said, “God has vindicated me; yes, he has heard me and given me a son,” so she named him Dan. 
>
><sup>7</sup> Rachel’s slave Bilhah conceived again and bore Jacob a second son. 
>
><sup>8</sup> Rachel said, “In my wrestlings with God, I have wrestled with my sister and won,” and she named him Naphtali. 
>
><sup>9</sup> When Leah saw that she had stopped having children, she took her slave Zilpah and gave her to Jacob as a wife. 
>
><sup>10</sup> Leah’s slave Zilpah bore Jacob a son. 
>
><sup>11</sup> Then Leah said, “What good fortune!” and she named him Gad. 
>
><sup>12</sup> When Leah’s slave Zilpah bore Jacob a second son, 
>
><sup>13</sup> Leah said, “I am happy that the women call me happy,” so she named him Asher. 
>
><sup>14</sup> Reuben went out during the wheat harvest and found some mandrakes in the field. When he brought them to his mother Leah, Rachel asked, “Please give me some of your son’s mandrakes.” 
>
><sup>15</sup> But Leah replied to her, “Isn’t it enough that you have taken my husband? Now you also want to take my son’s mandrakes?” “Well then,” Rachel said, “he can sleep with you tonight in exchange for your son’s mandrakes.” 
>
><sup>16</sup> When Jacob came in from the field that evening, Leah went out to meet him and said, “You must come with me, for I have hired you with my son’s mandrakes.” So Jacob slept with her that night. 
>
><sup>17</sup> God listened to Leah, and she conceived and bore Jacob a fifth son. 
>
><sup>18</sup> Leah said, “God has rewarded me for giving my slave to my husband,” and she named him Issachar. 
>
><sup>19</sup> Then Leah conceived again and bore Jacob a sixth son. 
>
><sup>20</sup> “God has given me a good gift,” Leah said. “This time my husband will honor me because I have borne six sons for him,” and she named him Zebulun. 
>
><sup>21</sup> Later, Leah bore a daughter and named her Dinah. 
>
><sup>22</sup> Then God remembered Rachel. He listened to her and opened her womb. 
>
><sup>23</sup> She conceived and bore a son, and she said, “God has taken away my disgrace.” 
>
><sup>24</sup> She named him Joseph and said, “May the Lord add another son to me.”Jacob’s Flocks Multiply
