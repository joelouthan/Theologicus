# Genesis 26:26-35 The Gospel Witness

## The Text

>Genesis 26:26–35  
><sup>26</sup> Now Abimelech came to him from Gerar with Ahuzzath his adviser and Phicol the commander of his army. 
>
><sup>27</sup> Isaac said to them, “Why have you come to me? You hated me and sent me away from you.” 
>
><sup>28</sup> They replied, “We have clearly seen how the Lord has been with you. We think there should be an oath between two parties—between us and you. Let us make a covenant with you: 
>
><sup>29</sup> You will not harm us, just as we have not harmed you but have done only what was good to you, sending you away in peace. You are now blessed by the Lord.” 
>
><sup>30</sup> So he prepared a banquet for them, and they ate and drank. 
>
><sup>31</sup> They got up early in the morning and swore an oath to each other. Isaac sent them on their way, and they left him in peace. 
>
><sup>32</sup> On that same day Isaac’s servants came to tell him about the well they had dug, saying to him, “We have found water!” 
>
><sup>33</sup> He called it Sheba. Therefore the name of the city is still Beer-sheba today.Esau’s Wives 
>
><sup>34</sup> When Esau was forty years old, he took as his wives Judith daughter of Beeri the Hethite, and Basemath daughter of Elon the Hethite. 
>
><sup>35</sup> They made life bitter for Isaac and Rebekah.
